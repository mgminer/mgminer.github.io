I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Oslo. I hold a PhD in Political Science from the University of California, Davis, and was previously a Research Fellow (2024–2025) at the Centre for International Security at the Hertie School in Berlin.

My research explores how new technologies are reshaping the relationship between states and citizens in contentious politics. I study both how states use tools like internet shutdowns, surveillance, and disinformation to suppress dissent, and how activists adapt their protest strategies in increasingly digital—and repressive—environments. With a regional focus on India and sub-Saharan Africa, my work sheds light on the evolving dynamics of mobilization, repression, and resistance in the 21st century.

At its core, my research asks: How do states and citizens compete for control in the digital age?
